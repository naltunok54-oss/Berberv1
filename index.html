Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This application ("the App") is designed to help barbers and salon professionals manage their daily appointments. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling any information collected through the App responsibly.

2. Data We Collect

The App stores the following information locally on your device: customer names, phone numbers, appointment times, and service history. No data is transmitted to any external server. All information remains solely on your device.

3. Local Storage

All data is saved using your browser's localStorage feature. This means your data stays on your device and is never shared with third parties. Clearing your browser data or app cache will permanently delete all stored records.

4. Phone Numbers

Phone numbers entered into the App are used exclusively for in-app calling and messaging via your device's native phone and SMS applications. We do not collect, transmit, or store phone numbers on any external system.

5. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any customer data with third parties. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any remote servers, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's own security measures. We recommend using a device passcode or biometric lock to protect your appointment records.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete individual records at any time using the trash (delete) function within the App. To remove all data, you may clear the App's local storage through your browser or device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by business professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer directly.
