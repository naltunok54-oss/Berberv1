Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This application is designed to help barbers manage appointments. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Collect

Customer names, phone numbers, appointment times stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any external server.

3. Local Storage

All data is saved using localStorage. Clearing your browser data will permanently delete all stored records.

4. Phone Numbers

Phone numbers are used exclusively for in-app calling and messaging via native phone/SMS apps.

5. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share any customer data with third parties.

6. Data Security

Data security depends on your device's own security measures.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete individual records at any time using the trash function.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer.
